Common questions

Pricing, answered.

Is hosting included in the price?

The package price covers design and development. Domain and hosting are billed separately at a flat annual rate, which we set up on your behalf so your site is live from day one.

How long does a project take?

Most Landing Page and Basic Website projects launch within 1–2 weeks. Business, Premium, and E-Commerce builds typically take 3–5 weeks depending on scope and content readiness.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Yes. Many clients start with a Landing Page or Basic Website and upgrade to a Business or Premium build as their needs grow — we'll only charge the difference in scope.

What happens after the support period ends?

Your site keeps running as normal. If you'd like ongoing updates, fixes, or improvements beyond the included support window, we offer simple monthly maintenance plans.
