How much does a website cost in Malaysia? A plain-English guide

What actually drives the price of a business website, what you get at each budget, and how to avoid paying for things you don't need.

By Danial Remy2 Nov 20265 min read

"How much for a website?" is the first question almost every business owner asks — and the honest answer is it depends on what the website has to do. A one-page site that collects enquiries is a very different job from an online store with payments and stock.

What decides the price

  • Number of pages. A single landing page is the fastest and cheapest. A full business site (Home, About, Services, Contact and more) takes more design and writing.
  • Custom design or template. A template is quicker and cheaper but looks like everyone else's. A custom design is built around your brand and how your customers actually decide.
  • Features. Contact forms are simple. Online payments, booking systems, customer logins and product catalogues add real development work.
  • Content. Photos, copywriting and logo work either come from you or add to the cost.
  • Speed and SEO. A site built cleanly — fast, mobile-friendly, properly structured — costs a little more to build and pays for itself in search visibility and enquiries.

What to expect at each level

At Rovexa, pricing starts from RM399 for a landing page, RM1,299 for a business website and RM1,999 for an e-commerce store. Those are starting points — the final quote depends on the factors above, and you can see the breakdown on our pricing page.

Rule of thumb

Pick the smallest website that does the job today, built in a way that lets you add pages and features later. You can always grow it — you can't get back money spent on features nobody uses.

Costs people forget about

The build price is only part of the picture. Budget for these as well:

  • Domain name (yearly renewal).
  • Hosting (yearly or monthly, depending on the provider).
  • Maintenance — updates, backups, security and small content changes, so the site doesn't quietly break or go out of date.

Questions to ask before you hire anyone

  1. What exactly is included — pages, revisions, mobile design, basic SEO?
  2. Who owns the website and domain once it's finished?
  3. What does it cost to make changes after launch?
  4. How long will it take, and what do you need from me?

A good studio will answer all four clearly, in writing, before you pay anything.
