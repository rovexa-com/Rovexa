Website or custom system? How to know when your business needs more

A website gets customers to find you. A custom system helps you run the business behind it. Here's how to tell which one you need.

By Danial Remy2 Nov 20265 min read

A website and a custom business system do two different jobs. A website helps customers find and trust you. A system helps you run the work behind the scenes — bookings, jobs, customers, staff and reports — without a tangle of spreadsheets and chat threads.

Signs a website is enough

  • Your main goal is to get found on Google and receive enquiries.
  • You handle bookings and customers comfortably by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • You have a small team and a manageable number of jobs each week.

Signs you've outgrown spreadsheets and chat

  • You've double-booked a job, or forgotten to follow one up.
  • You can't quickly tell which jobs are pending, in progress or completed.
  • Customer details and history live in several places.
  • You're unsure which staff member is doing what on a given day.
  • Working out monthly revenue or your most popular services takes hours.

What a custom system can look like

Take a cleaning company as an example. One dashboard can show today's bookings and upcoming appointments, let you create a booking in under a minute, track every job's status, keep a searchable customer list, show each staff member's assignments, and report on revenue and top services. You can see exactly this in the demo walkthrough on our homepage.

Start small

You don't have to build everything at once. Many businesses launch a website first, then add a booking or customer portal once they know which tasks eat the most time.

How to decide

Write down the three tasks that take up the most of your week. If they involve copying information between places, chasing updates, or keeping track of who's doing what, a system could save you real time. If they're mostly about getting more customers, put your effort into the website first.

Not sure which side you're on? Tell us how your business runs and we'll give you an honest recommendation — even if the answer is "you just need a good website".
