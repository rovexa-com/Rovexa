01 — The studio

We believe a home should feel effortless — never staged. Every room we make begins with listening, is shaped by climate and craft, and is finished only when it feels like it has always belonged to you.

RUANG is Malay for space — and for the room, the pause and the breath between things. For more than a decade our team of designers, joiners and project managers has shaped family homes, restored timber houses and fitted out boutique spaces across Malaysia.

From the first conversation to the last cushion, you work with one accountable team, one honest budget and one clear point of view.

03 — What we do

One studio, every layer of the room

Architecture, joinery, lighting and styling are rarely done by the same hands. At RUANG they are.

01

Residential Interiors

Full-home design from first sketch to final styling — layouts, finishes, furniture and lighting, delivered as a single considered whole.

02

Heritage Restoration

Sensitive repair and reimagining of timber houses and shophouses, with original joinery, carving and character carefully retained.

03

Bespoke Joinery

Cabinetry, wall panelling and furniture drawn by us and built in our own workshop — made to fit your room and your life.

04

Biophilic & Garden Rooms

Courtyards, water features and planting schemes that cool a home naturally and bring the tropics inside.

05

Lighting & Materials

Layered lighting plans and a tactile palette of stone, timber, brass and textile, sourced from trusted makers.

06

Styling & Curation

Art, objects, books and plants — the final five per cent that makes a finished room feel inhabited.

04 — How we work

A calm process, in four movements

i.

Listen

We spend time in your home and with your family, learning how you live before a single line is drawn.

ii.

Draw

Plans, moodboards and materials come together in a concept you can see, touch and respond to.

iii.

Make

Our workshop and trusted trades build the details — and we manage every site visit ourselves.

iv.

Settle

We style, hand over and stay close, with aftercare that continues long after the keys are in your hand.
